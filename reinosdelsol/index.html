Help

Lead Solhaven, a small oasis town in the Ember Dunes, and grow it into a fortified city. Give every citizen a job, build on the right ground (farms only grow on the green oasis), research technologies, train soldiers and defend against raids. Every soldier is a citizen who stops working.

Controls

Drag to move the map and pinch to zoom. Tap a building to open it. Tap a soldier (or Select for your whole army), then tap the ground to move or an enemy to attack. You can pause at any time and still give orders.

What is free?

Act 1 (missions 1–4), Free Build and the Daily puzzle are free. The Full Campaign is a one-time purchase that unlocks Act 2 (missions 5–8). To restore it on a new device: Settings › Restore purchases. There are no ads, no energy and no timers.

How do I change the language?

The game is in 44 languages and follows your iPhone's language. To change it for this game only: iOS Settings › Apps › Reinos del Sol › Language.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

We don't collect personal data that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device; deleting the app deletes them. The game does not use the microphone, camera, location, photos or contacts.

Gameplay statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To improve the game we use Google Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config and Crashlytics). With your permission it receives gameplay data (for example missions played, time, stars and choices made), technical and crash data, and an app installation identifier. It never includes your name or anything about you, is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland nothing is sent until you say yes; everywhere you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Share anonymous gameplay statistics. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The Full Campaign is bought through Apple. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your payment details.

Español

Ayuda

Diriges Solhaven, un pequeño pueblo de oasis en las Dunas de Ascua, y lo conviertes en una ciudad fortificada. Da trabajo a cada habitante, construye en el terreno correcto (las granjas solo crecen en el oasis verde), investiga, entrena soldados y defiéndete de las incursiones. Cada soldado es un habitante que deja de trabajar.

Arrastra para mover el mapa y pellizca para hacer zoom. Toca un edificio para abrirlo. Toca un soldado (o Elegir para todo tu ejército) y luego el suelo para moverlo o un enemigo para atacar. Puedes pausar en cualquier momento y seguir dando órdenes.

El Acto 1 (misiones 1–4), la Construcción libre y el Reto diario son gratis. La Campaña completa es una compra única que desbloquea el Acto 2 (misiones 5–8). Para restaurarla: Ajustes › Restaurar compras. Sin anuncios, sin energía y sin esperas.
